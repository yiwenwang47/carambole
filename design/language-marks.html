Carambole · language marks

Two flat marks, drawn with a compass

A new start. Each language gets a flat geometric mark, built only from circles and arcs: no metal, no 3D, no glow. Français keeps the idea of one ribbon link: a lens pinched at both ends, with a twist. English keeps the idea of a petal. Six ways to draw the pair are below, each taken from a lesson in your references. Pick one card to see it on two new home pages.
