Overflow choices

Three places where text could outgrow its space. Each row shows how it looks today, then the options. Real app data at 390 pt, the narrowest phone width. Tap the Leçon rows to arm or disarm them. Tap the « Trop facile » label to try each armed state.

1 · Leçon row when armed

When a row is armed, the « Trop facile » label shares the headword's line. A long expression then shrinks: « faire la connaissance de » drops to 81 %, and the floor is 70 %. Leçon 64, last five rows. Two rows are armed here.

Todaylabel beside, headword shrinks

    Safe for the current 1,200 words (worst case 0.81). If a new expression needed less than 0.7, nothing else would catch it.

    Alabel under the gloss

      The headword line stays the same as on an unarmed row, so it never shrinks. The armed row gains one short line.

      Bheadword wraps

        The label stays beside the headword. A long armed headword breaks onto two lines at full size; the part of speech and « Trop facile » sit on the first line's baseline.

        2 · Book 1 and 2: Trop facile footer (decided: keep today's layout)

        You dropped « Réponds correctement pour le retirer ». That line also held « Annuler » (disarm before answering) and, after a miss, « Raté — remis à zéro ». What should the label do once it's armed? Book 1 card shown. Book 2 uses the same control.

        Alabel toggles

        Tap to arm: the label turns ink on a pale pill and reads « Trop facile — annuler ». Tap again to disarm. After a miss the label goes away, because the card is already set to be reset.

        Barmed until answered

        Tap to arm: the label turns ink and reads « Trop facile — armé ». It can't be undone before answering. The undo pill after a clean answer stays. After a miss the label goes away.

        3 · Word tiles (Book 1 and 2)

        Tiles are one line. The widest real tile, « Est-ce que vous parlez anglais? », is 270 of 300 pt. To show the risk, the option phones swap it for a made-up longer tile as the first one: « Est-ce que vous parlez anglais avec eux? ».

        Todayreal tiles

        The real drill (el_questions_inversion_05). Everything fits with 30 pt to spare.

        Alint guard

        The app doesn't change. The Book 1 and Book 2 lints measure every tile and fail on one wider than its tray, so it gets rewritten or split before it ships.

        Bwrap to two lines

        A long tile becomes a two-line block at full size. Its corners drop to 20 pt (from a full capsule) and it gets 7 pt padding top and bottom. Rows of tiles no longer share one height.

        B′wrap, capsule kept

        Same 1 pt ink border and fully round ends as the other tiles, with 20 pt side padding so the text clears the curve. A tile wraps only when it can't fit on one line; every other tile stays one line.

        Cshrink to fit

        The long tile's text scales down (here 0.87), with a floor of 0.8 that the lint checks. That tile reads smaller than its neighbours.
