Palettes, round three

Round one copied the moodboard's colours. This round takes four lessons from it instead and builds every palette from them. The star and the library ribbon are now the real 3D objects: the star uses the app's extrude numbers, and the ribbon is LibraryRibbon.swift ported line for line, fed with your phone's last 30 days. Drag a star to turn it.

Colour is a materialThe lime keychain glows because light passes through acrylic; the star sticker is chrome. Colour lives in what the object is made of, not in a flat fill.
One loud, one quietEvery image pairs a sharp hue with a soft one of the opposite temperature: lime and sky, yellow and black, mauve and brown.
Black does the drawingType and line work are always black. Colour never carries text or outlines; it sits behind or inside them.
OverprintOn the poster, two inks cross and make a third. Where coloured things overlap here, they multiply instead of covering each other.
