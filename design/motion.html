The drill keeps its standard layout (prompt, answer, fixed button), but the words become magnets. The bank is a level row of word tiles. Tap a word and it springs onto the line with a little overshoot; tap it again and it flies home. When the answer is right, the magnets weld into one sentence, the leftover words drop off the screen, and a single weight wave runs through it (the 02 motion). When it’s wrong, the line shakes and the wrong words turn dashed. Try both. iOS routeSwiftUI only: each token is a view with .position driven by withAnimation(.spring(duration: .35, bounce: .3)), a matchedGeometryEffect between bank and line, .phaseAnimator for the shake, and the 02 wght wave on success. Light haptic per snap.

Bibliothèque

One paper ribbon runs down the whole shelf, and each book is a stretch of it. The ribbon is drawn inside the scrolling page, so it moves exactly with the titles. A book you have started is drawn from its last 30 days (width = work, a full twist = a rest day); a full twist also marks where one book ends and the next begins. Books to come are a narrow, faint strip of tracing paper. With 10 books the shelf simply gets longer: each book takes the same short stretch (≈ 200 pt), started books first, so the live part of the ribbon is always on the first screen. iOS routeThe ribbon mesh in a RealityView (orthographic camera) placed inside the ScrollView content, as tall as the list, so the system scroll moves it; only its gentle sway animates.
