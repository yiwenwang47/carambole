The ribbon is the history timeline: oldest day at the top, today at the bottom. Width is how much you did that day, and a rest day pinches it into a half-twist that shows the grey back of the paper. Run a finger down it to read any day, and drag sideways to turn it. Big weeks look wide and generous, and broken streaks look like knots. iOS routeRealityKit procedural MeshResource (≈2k vertices), a DragGesture mapped to a day index with a selection haptic per day, and a SwiftUI overlay for the readout.

The table of contents is the fruit itself. Cut a carambole and every slice is a star, so the book is 25 star slices stacked on a skewer, module 01 at the bottom. Mastered modules are chrome (the same metal as the 01 object), modules in progress are white, and the ones still to come are faint glass outlines. The column literally grows as you learn. How to use it: slide a finger up or down the stack and the slice under your finger fans out, with a haptic tick per module and its name at the bottom. Let go to keep it chosen, drag sideways to turn the stack, and tap (or “Ouvrir”) to open: the slice lifts out, turns to face you and grows into the module page. Same gesture as scrubbing the 05 ribbon. This could be what “swipe up” on the 01 book home reveals: the single chrome star splits into its 25 slices. iOS routeRealityKit (or SceneKit) with 25 instances of the same extruded star mesh and three materials. A vertical DragGesture maps to a module index with a selection haptic per slice; the fan-open gap is a spring on each slice’s y. SwiftUI overlay for the readout.

Construis la phrase
« I’m going to leave tomorrow. »

The drill keeps its standard layout (prompt, answer, fixed button), but the words become magnets. The bank is a level row of word tiles. Tap a word and it springs onto the line with a little overshoot; tap it again and it flies home. When the answer is right, the magnets weld into one sentence, the leftover words drop off the screen, and a single weight wave runs through it (the 02 motion). When it’s wrong, the line shakes and the wrong words turn dashed. Try both. iOS routeSwiftUI only: each token is a view with .position driven by withAnimation(.spring(duration: .35, bounce: .3)), a matchedGeometryEffect between bank and line, .phaseAnimator for the shake, and the 02 wght wave on success. Light haptic per snap.
