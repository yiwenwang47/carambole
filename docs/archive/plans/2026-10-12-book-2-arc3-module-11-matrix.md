# Book 2, module 11 *Grève et retards*: content matrix

**Arc:** Les imprévus · **Cast:** Station staff, announcements, a taxi driver and other travellers (vous); Léa, Inès, Théo, Karim (tu); M. Ferrand, Mme Dumas, Robin's manager (vous) · **Goal:** Understand a strike, a cancellation or a delay, and find another way to get there.

Paper plan only: no French is authored yet. Overview, shared base glosses, rules and open questions: [arc 3 plan](2026-10-12-book-2-arc3-plan.md). The format follows the arc 2 matrices.

Each move is one element `sit_11_<move>` with 15 items in the fixed order d01 d02 s01 d03 d04 s02 … d09 d10 s05. Drill codes: `U` Comprendre, `R` Répondre, `C` Compléter, `M` Mot (target in brackets), `P` Choisir, `F` Corriger. Standalone drills run Construire, Choisir le ton, Corriger, Transformer, Mot en retour, in that order. **New** = first taught book-wide in this item (its `firstSeen`); it is always printed in a turn of that item (a server line or a printed learner line), never only in a gap or a built reply. **Reuse** = words taught earlier in the book (arcs 1–2, café, or earlier in this batch) that recur in the item; *Fondations* base words are not listed. A cue starting *Clarify* is the move's clarification or repetition dialogue; it is always face to face or a live call, never a text, WhatsApp or voicemail. In a Fill sketch (Compléter and Mot en retour) the **first** choice is the correct one; vary its slot when authoring. A model reply in brackets is a sketch: accept every valid order and the with/without « Pardon » variants. Quoted French is a sketch for the author, not final text and never cue text. The English cue names the speaker, states tu or vous (« vous = you all » in the group chat) and says "You're a woman." where the answer hinges on it; standalone `place` labels below already match the cue's setting. The Cast column gives an unnamed speaker's `role` (and its voice: male, or `voice: "female"`); `name` stays empty.

## Grammar recycled from *Fondations*

*il y a* + noun without article (*il y a grève*) and *aucun* + noun (05 negation); agreement of past participles used as adjectives (*perturbé / perturbée*, *annulé / annulée*, *prévu / prévue*, 07); passé composé with *être* (« le train est parti », and Robin's feminine *je suis arrivée / rentrée*, 14); futur proche (« je vais être en retard », « je vais échanger mon billet », 12); questions with *quel / quelle / à quelle heure / quand* (06); *vous* and *tu* imperatives (*prenez / prends*, *revenez*, 20); object pronouns *je te tiens / je vous tiens au courant*, *je la fais* (17); quantity + *de* (*vingt minutes de retard*, 11); *en* + transport or state (*en navette, en télétravail*) against *à pied / à vélo* (10).
