# Gloss findings — module 10 (Se repérer)

- [MUST] sit_10_turn_d03, sit_10_turn_d04 — tourne — « Je tourne où ? » / « Je tourne à droite ? » show tourne = "turn (tu) / turns". The person note says tu in a je sentence, so the learner reads it as the wrong form (the module's own feedback teaches « tourne » = tu order). → FIX: widen key: "turn (tu) / turns" → "turn (je, tu) / turns". Grep: the other 11 « tourne » uses are tu orders (« Tourne à gauche », « Non, tourne à gauche ! »), bank tiles and feedback text. The new gloss fits all of them. It also matches the style of prends = "take / have (je, tu)".
- [SHOULD] sit_10_landmark_d08 (bank tile « il y a cinq minutes ! »), plus the « il y a dix minutes. » tile in another module — il y a — this distractor means "five minutes ago", but the gloss "there is / there are" reads as "there are five minutes". By the book's convention, a real French distractor keeps its real meaning, and that meaning is "ago". → FIX: widen key: "there is / there are" → "there is / there are (+ time: ago)". Grep: the 47 « il y a + number » lines are all "there is/are" except these two tiles. « Il y a vingt minutes de retard » still fits.
- [SHOULD] sit_10_door_d05 — sonne — « Je sonne à votre interphone » shows sonne = "rings". That is third person ("I rings"). → FIX: widen key: "rings" → "ring (je) / rings". The only other use is « mon réveil ne sonne pas », which still fits.
- [SHOULD] sit_10_lost_d07, sit_10_landmark_d03, sit_10_landmark_d07 — la (in « je la vois ») — the first sense, "the (feminine)", contradicts the sentence: "I · the · see". Here la is the object pronoun "it". → FIX: add key {"fr": "je la vois", "en": "I see it / I see her", "spoken": false, "audio": "sitw_je_la_vois", "firstSeen": null}. Grep: these 3 lines are the only uses of « je la vois » in the book.
- [SHOULD] sit_10_landmark_d04, sit_10_turn_d09, sit_10_transit_d03, sit_10_transit_d08 (and about 118 lines book-wide) — chez + moi / toi — « Je passe chez toi », « devant chez moi », « Pour venir chez moi » are split into "at (someone's place) · me / you". This does not read as "my place / your place". The key « chez vous » already exists, but « chez moi » and « chez toi » do not. → FIX: add key {"fr": "chez moi", "en": "(at / to) my place, home", "spoken": false, "audio": "sitw_chez_moi", "firstSeen": null} and {"fr": "chez toi", "en": "(at / to) your place, home (tu)", "spoken": false, "audio": "sitw_chez_toi", "firstSeen": null}. The longer key « fais comme chez toi » still wins. « de chez moi » and « en face de chez toi » segment correctly.

- [SHOULD] sit_10_ask_d02, sit_10_ask_d06, sit_10_ask_d07, sit_10_ask_d09, sit_10_ask_d10, sit_10_ask_s03 — de (after « je me suis trompé(e) ») — « je me suis trompée de rue / de chemin » reads "I went the wrong way · of / from · street". Widen key « trompé », « trompée », « trompés » the same way with "(se tromper de … = to take the wrong …)".
