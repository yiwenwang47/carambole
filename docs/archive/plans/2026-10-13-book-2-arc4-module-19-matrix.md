# Book 2, module 19 *Choisir ensemble*: content matrix

**Arc:** Raconter · **Cast:** Théo, Léa, Inès, Karim (tu; vous = you all in the group); the restaurant, Robin's manager and Mme Dumas (vous) · **Goal:** Propose, compare options and negotiate a plan in the group chat, then confirm it and share the costs.

Paper plan only: no French is authored yet. Overview, shared base glosses, rules and open questions: [arc 4 plan](2026-10-13-book-2-arc4-plan.md). The format follows the arc 3 matrices.

Each move is one element `sit_19_<move>` with 15 items in the fixed order d01 d02 s01 d03 d04 s02 … d09 d10 s05. Drill codes: `U` Comprendre, `R` Répondre, `C` Compléter, `M` Mot (target in brackets), `P` Choisir, `F` Corriger. Standalone drills run Construire, Choisir le ton, Corriger, Transformer, Mot en retour, in that order. **New** = first taught book-wide in this item (its `firstSeen`); it is always printed in a turn of that item (a server line or a printed learner line), never only in a gap or a built reply. **Reuse** = words taught earlier in the book (modules 01–15, or earlier in this batch) that recur in the item; *Fondations* base words are not listed. A cue starting *Clarify* is the move's clarification or repetition dialogue; it is always face to face or a live call, never a text, the group chat or WhatsApp. In a Fill sketch (Compléter and Mot en retour) the **first** choice is the correct one; vary its slot when authoring. A model reply in brackets is a sketch: accept every valid order and the with/without « Pardon » variants. Quoted French is a sketch for the author, not final text and never cue text. The English cue names the speaker, states tu or vous (« vous = you all » in the group chat) and says "You're a woman." where the answer hinges on it; standalone `place` labels below already match the cue's setting. The Cast column gives an unnamed speaker's `role` (and its voice: male, or `voice: "female"`); `name` stays empty. Comprendre questions are not sketched: the author writes them, and **checks every `correct` index against the true choice** (arc 3 shipped 15 inverted keys).

## Grammar recycled from *Fondations*

conditional for soft proposals and preferences *on pourrait*, *je préférerais*, *ça vous dirait* (22; Corriger on *on pourrais*, *je préférerait*); *vous* plural to the group against *tu* to one friend (*ça vous dit / ça te dit*, *comme vous voulez / comme tu veux*, 03); *il faut* + infinitive (*il faut voter*); passé composé *j'ai voté*, *la majorité a préféré*, *j'ai réservé* (13); *chacun* + singular verb (Corriger on *choisissent*); futur simple *je serai là* (21); object pronouns *je te rembourse*, *je te l'envoie*, *on le prend* (17); *y* in *on y va* (18); *de + le = du* (*à côté du pont*, 10); numbers stay small words (*cinq, huit, dix, vingt*).
