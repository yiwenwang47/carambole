# Au café: second-half content matrix

Rewritten 2026-10-11 after the independent review and the Fondations vocabulary audit. The learner is new to Lyon. Each cue states whether the speaker says tu or vous. Karim says vous in takeaway, mixup and pay; in `usual` he says vous in d01–d03 and switches to tu in d04 (« On peut se tutoyer, non ? »); later `usual` cues say "Karim switched to tu with you last week". Order within each move is D D S, repeated five times.

`New` lists words first taught in that item (`firstSeen`). Every new word recurs in at least two later dialogues and has a later Mot or Mot en retour drill (checked by `lint.py` on the assembled module). All other glossed words are Fondations grammar forms or base A1 words; café and payment terms are taught, not assumed. The payment move avoids *payer* (untaught) and uses *par carte*, *en espèces*, *ça fait*, *monnaie* (= change, one sense throughout).
