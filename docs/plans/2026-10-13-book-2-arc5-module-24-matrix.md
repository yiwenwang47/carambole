# Book 2, module 24 *Et si… ?*: content matrix

**Arc:** Échanger · **Cast:** Théo, Inès, Léa, Karim (tu); Robin's manager, M. Ferrand, Mme Dumas, station staff and the gîte owner (vous) · **Goal:** Plan a long weekend: suggest with « et si on… », weigh the weather, make a plan B, book and budget, and decide.

Paper plan only: no French is authored yet. Overview, shared base glosses, gloss decisions, rules and open questions: [arc 5 plan](2026-10-13-book-2-arc5-plan.md). The format follows the arc 4 matrices.

Each move is one element `sit_24_<move>` with 15 items in the fixed order d01 d02 s01 d03 d04 s02 … d09 d10 s05. Drill codes: `U` Comprendre, `R` Répondre, `C` Compléter, `M` Mot (target in brackets), `P` Choisir, `F` Corriger. Standalone drills run Construire, Choisir le ton, Corriger, Transformer, Mot en retour, in that order. **New** = first taught book-wide in this item (its `firstSeen`); it is always printed in a turn of that item (a server line or a printed learner line), never only in a gap or a built reply. **Reuse** = words taught earlier in the book (modules 01–20, or earlier in this batch) that recur in the item, distractors included; *Fondations* base words are not listed. A cue starting *Clarify* is the move's clarification or repetition dialogue; it is always face to face or a live call, never a text, the group chat or WhatsApp. In a Fill sketch (Compléter and Mot en retour) the **first** choice is the correct one; vary its slot when authoring. A model reply in brackets is a sketch: accept every valid order and the with/without « Pardon » variants. Quoted French is a sketch for the author, not final text and never cue text. The English cue names the speaker, states tu or vous (« vous = you all » in the group chat) and says "You're a woman." where the answer hinges on it; standalone `place` labels below already match the cue's setting. The Cast column gives an unnamed speaker's `role` (and its voice: male, or `voice: "female"`); `name` stays empty. Comprendre questions are not sketched: the author writes them, and **checks every `correct` index against the true choice**. **Glosses:** every quoted token was segmented longest-key-first against `words.json`, this batch's keys and the tables below, the way the app splits it (0 unglossed); before handing in, the author runs `gloss_dump.py` on the draft and reads every token's gloss against its sentence (plan §2.8).

## Grammar recycled from *Fondations*

*si* + present + futur simple *s'il fait beau, on ira au lac* (25 with 21; Transformer); *si* + imparfait + conditional *si on avait une voiture, on irait à Annecy* (25 with 22; Transformer, Corriger on *irais*); the suggestion *et si on* + imparfait *et si on partait ?* (25: *si on allait au cinéma*; Transformer); futur simple *on restera, on regardera, je ferai* (21); conditional *je voudrais louer, vous pourriez, ce serait* (22); *il faut* + infinitive and *on peut / on pourrait* (22); *y* in *on y va* (18); *de toute façon / au pire* for alternatives.
