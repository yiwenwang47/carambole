# Book 2, module 17 *Une galère*: content matrix

**Arc:** Raconter · **Cast:** Théo, Léa, Inès, Karim (tu); M. Ferrand, Mme Dumas and Robin's manager (vous) · **Goal:** React to a friend's bad-day story, sympathise, ask what happened and ask back.

Paper plan only: no French is authored yet. Overview, shared base glosses, rules and open questions: [arc 4 plan](2026-10-13-book-2-arc4-plan.md). The format follows the arc 3 matrices.

Each move is one element `sit_17_<move>` with 15 items in the fixed order d01 d02 s01 d03 d04 s02 … d09 d10 s05. Drill codes: `U` Comprendre, `R` Répondre, `C` Compléter, `M` Mot (target in brackets), `P` Choisir, `F` Corriger. Standalone drills run Construire, Choisir le ton, Corriger, Transformer, Mot en retour, in that order. **New** = first taught book-wide in this item (its `firstSeen`); it is always printed in a turn of that item (a server line or a printed learner line), never only in a gap or a built reply. **Reuse** = words taught earlier in the book (modules 01–15, or earlier in this batch) that recur in the item; *Fondations* base words are not listed. A cue starting *Clarify* is the move's clarification or repetition dialogue; it is always face to face or a live call, never a text, the group chat or WhatsApp. In a Fill sketch (Compléter and Mot en retour) the **first** choice is the correct one; vary its slot when authoring. A model reply in brackets is a sketch: accept every valid order and the with/without « Pardon » variants. Quoted French is a sketch for the author, not final text and never cue text. The English cue names the speaker, states tu or vous (« vous = you all » in the group chat) and says "You're a woman." where the answer hinges on it; standalone `place` labels below already match the cue's setting. The Cast column gives an unnamed speaker's `role` (and its voice: male, or `voice: "female"`); `name` stays empty. Comprendre questions are not sketched: the author writes them, and **checks every `correct` index against the true choice** (arc 3 shipped 15 inverted keys).

## Grammar recycled from *Fondations*

passé composé with *être* in a story (*il est tombé*, Robin *je suis tombée*: Corriger on *tombé*, 14) and *avoir* (*j'ai raté*, *j'ai oublié*, 13); imparfait for the background and passé composé for the event (*il pleuvait et j'ai oublié mon parapluie*; *tout à coup, mon vélo ne marchait plus*, 16); pronominal passé composé *qu'est-ce qui s'est passé ?* (15); futur proche *ça va aller*, *ça va s'arranger* (12); comparison *pire que la tienne* (19, with *la tienne* from its examples); adjective agreement *dur / dure*, *énervant*, *trempé / trempée*, *mon pauvre / ma pauvre* (07); *quel / quelle* (06); *tu* and *vous* imperatives *repose-toi / reposez-vous*, *ne t'inquiète pas / ne vous inquiétez pas* (20); *ne… plus* (*plus de batterie*, 05); object pronouns *je te prête*, *je te donne un coup de main*, *je t'invite* (17).
