# Book 2 direction

**Decision (2026-10-10):** Remove the *Intermédiaire* placeholder from the library. Keep one placeholder for Book 2 while its final title and scope are decided.

The preferred direction is **En situation**: a practical follow-up to *Fondations* that uses its A1–B1 grammar in connected, everyday exchanges. Organize lessons around situations such as making plans, ordering, getting around, telling a story, explaining a problem, and giving an opinion. Each lesson should include listening to a short exchange and forming a useful response. Revisit grammar in context instead of starting another grammar sequence.

The existing *Conversation* shelf label remains provisional. Decide the title, syllabus, and exercise design before building the book. *Intermédiaire* (B1→B2) is not part of the current library plan.
