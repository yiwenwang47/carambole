Pilot · module 1, group 1

Quand le concert s'arrête

Group note: « Repère la panne et l'action qui était déjà en cours. » Its 5 current cards (22 to 33 words each) become 9 cards. Each card has one or two short sentences at the top. Every option is French. Trop facile sits just above four tiles at the bottom; a tap grades at once. A typed card shows the English and the infinitive under the sentence; you type on an underline above the keyboard and check with Vérifier. The keyboard is Apple's French AZERTY layout: hold e, a, u, i, o or c for accents; the apostrophe sits beside n.

Book 1 today (simulator)

Book 1 keeps prompt text ink and puts colour on objects. Book 3 now borrows each one:

  • Placed magnet → the blank. The blank is a pale lime well inside the sentence; the answer lands in it as a lime magnet.
  • Bank tray → the options. Four outlined tiles sit on the same pale blue tray as Book 1's word bank.
  • Indice disc → the group note. Tap it for « Repère la panne… » on a pale card, like Book 1's hint.
  • Header and gloss. Drill type and count centred, progress ring on the right, group name as the small label, English cue in grey quotes.
  • Écouter. Same disc as Book 1, on the right. It stays faded until the blank is answered, as in Book 2, then plays the whole sentence with the answer in it. Tap it after answering (this page uses your browser's French voice; the app would ship recorded clips).
  • Feedback. « Juste ✓ » in an outlined pill, as in Book 1. A miss gets a pale card with the reason.
Ambiguity guard

Cue words close off the other tense

« Léa a parlé à Théo quand la musique s'est arrêtée » is also correct French. So card 5 carries « soudain », and typing cards give the English, which fixes the tense. Every blank needs this check: read the sentence with each wrong option filled in, and each must be wrong.

What was cut

Dialogue and story framing

Léa and Théo's two-line exchange and the 3-sentence microtext are gone. Their content survives as single sentences (cards 4 to 7). Nothing in this group needed more than one sentence to test.

Our own keyboard

Apple's layout, Carambole's look

Same AZERTY keys and accent popups as the iOS French keyboard, so nothing to relearn. Drawn on the palette's pale tray with white keys, and Vérifier as the return key. The layout never jumps as a system keyboard slides in. Costs: no dictation or third-party keyboards, and hardware-keyboard and VoiceOver support have to be built.

Cards 1 to 4 then drill each word with the English cue. Cards 5 to 9 reuse the words without English, inside grammar cards. The 6 current cards (32 to 82 words) tested reading and tact. Words first

See it, drill it with English, then reuse it

Each new word appears on the Leçon screen, then in one card with the English cue, then again without English in later cards (« délai » in cards 1, 5 and 6). Wrong options are mostly known look-alikes (retard, prétendre), so the new word is never one guess among four unknowns.

Grammar

Indirect questions, in reuse cards

« si », not « est-ce que »; « quand il sera », not « sera-t-il »; the question word that matches the missing detail. The Indice note now states the rule, not a reading tip.

One-answer check

A real idiom was caught by hand

« la marche à suivre » means "the procedure", so it was a second right answer for card 2. It is now « dérive ». Grammalecte flags only gender errors here (« le démarche »), so a human read is still the gate.

Open

Should new words count as elements?

The words are now taught on purpose, so FSRS could track each word as well as the group. Today it tracks only the group. This is for the content-model step.
